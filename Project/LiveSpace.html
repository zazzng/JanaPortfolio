Overview

Live Space is a mobile app designed to simplify shared living by giving building residents real-time visibility into shared amenities without having to leave their apartment to check.

The project stemmed from interviews with residents who described the same silent frustration: walking to the gym, laundry room, or party space only to find it occupied. Every failed trip stacked a small annoyance on top of the last, until the building's amenities felt more like gambles than conveniences.

Main Features

Check Amenities

See what's available in your building in real time, from laundry machines to parking spaces.

Book Amenities

Reserve shared spaces and amenities in advance so you can plan your day with confidence.

Move-in / Move-out

Streamline your move-in and move-out process with our easy-to-use tools and resources.

Get notifications

Stay informed about important updates and changes to your building or community.
