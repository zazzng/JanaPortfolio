Overview

Live Space is a mobile app designed to simplify shared living by giving building residents real-time visibility into shared amenities without having to leave their apartment to check.

Target Users

The app is designed for residents of multi-unit buildings who want to make the most of their shared amenities without the hassle of constantly checking their availability.

Pain Points

After interviewing 10 residents and 3 concierge staff, it became clear that the lack of real-time information about shared amenities was a major source of frustration.

Residents often face the frustration of discovering that shared amenities are unavailable when they arrive to use them, leading to wasted time and disappointment.

Gap in the Market

For all 3 the apartments and shared spaces we have researched, there is no solution that provides real-time visibility into shared amenities.

Solution

LiveSpace addresses this gap by providing a real-time view of shared amenities, allowing residents to make informed decisions about their use.

Main Features

Check Amenities

See what's available in your building in real time, from laundry machines to parking spaces.

Book Amenities

Reserve shared spaces and amenities in advance so you can plan your day with confidence.

Move-in / Move-out

Streamline your move-in and move-out process with our easy-to-use tools and resources.

Get notifications

Stay informed about important updates and changes to your building or community.

What I worked on

Conducted interviews that surfaced the three core pain points: guessing availability, friction in the booking process, and difficulty reaching building management

Designed the UX/UI across the full app

Built the project landing page in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
